Room setup

Small room setup ideas for students

Simple ways to make a small room more functional without expensive furniture or major changes.

Use vertical space

Small shelves, hooks and organizers can keep frequently used items off the main desk area. Avoid blocking ventilation or exits.

Keep one clear work surface

A clutter-free desk is often more useful than adding more furniture. Give every frequently used item a simple place.

Use lighting intentionally

Task lighting can improve usability without changing the whole room. Choose a light that suits your desk size and available power points.
