Laptop accessories

7 useful laptop accessories for college students

A practical shortlist of accessory categories that solve common portability, connectivity and study problems.

1. Laptop stand

Useful when you want to raise the screen or reclaim desk space.

2. External mouse

Helpful for long study or coding sessions if you dislike trackpad-only use.

3. USB hub

Worth considering when your laptop has limited ports. Match the hub to your actual devices.

4. Power extension

Choose a properly rated product from a reputable seller and follow electrical safety instructions.

5. Laptop sleeve or backpack

Prioritize fit, padding and comfortable carrying over unnecessary compartments.

6. Cleaning kit

A simple microfiber cloth and device-safe cleaning routine may be enough.

7. Headphones

Useful for shared rooms, online classes and focused study sessions.
